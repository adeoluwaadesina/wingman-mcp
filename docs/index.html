How it works

Describe what you're working on. Claude builds the plan.

The panel mounts inline in the conversation, right where you're already talking.

01

Ask for a plan

Tell Claude or ChatGPT what you're working on. It creates the plan and the panel mounts inline with tasks already populated.

02

Work through it together

Click a checkbox yourself, or let the AI tick tasks off as it finishes them. Drag to reorder, mark work in progress.

03

Pick up anywhere

The plan survives restarts and new conversations. With Wingman Cloud, it's on your phone too.
